# Tutor Application Triage — Méthode Secondaire

The public **Devenir tuteur** page is the intake point for candidates. Its form sends an application to the recruitment inbox. The CRM `Candidate Pipeline` tab is the operating record; do not use the inbox as a pipeline.

## Service level

- Review new applications every business day during the hiring period.
- Send a short acknowledgement or decision within one business day.
- Copy a viable candidate into the CRM on the day the application is reviewed.
- Never promise clients, hours, or a start date during first contact.

## Workflow

1. Review the website-form submission in the recruitment inbox.
2. Decline obvious mismatches courteously; do not add them to the CRM.
3. For every viable candidate, create one `Candidate Pipeline` row and record:
   - candidate ID, name, email, source `website`, target role, claimed subjects/levels, language, format, and recurring peak-time availability;
   - resume status, screening result, next action, due date, and current timestamp.
4. Screen against the [candidate scorecard](candidate-scorecard.md). Confirm their available slots are recurring rather than one-time.
5. Book an interview and mock lesson only when the candidate could fill a current gap.
6. Use the [interview and mock-lesson guide](interview-and-mock-lesson.md), record the score on the same day, and choose `advance`, `backup`, or `decline`.
7. Before an approval, complete the onboarding checklist and confirm their actual availability in the live CRM.

## Pipeline definitions

| Stage | Meaning | Required next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sourced` | Contact detail exists; no application yet | Request application or schedule screening |
| `screening` | Application reviewed | Score fit and availability |
| `interview` | Interview booked or completed | Book/complete mock lesson |
| `mock lesson` | Teaching sample pending review | Score within one business day |
| `checks` | Candidate meets quality threshold | Complete reference/safeguarding checks |
| `approved` | May be matched when slots fit | Add confirmed recurring availability |
| `backup` | Quality is suitable; demand or availability does not yet fit | Recheck monthly during the school year |
| `declined` | Does not meet the standard or current need | Close with a courteous response |

## Matching safeguard

Availability is intentionally editable by the tutor team. A CRM entry is a planning input, not a guarantee. Before a family is offered a time, confirm the tutor's live availability and record the confirmed recurring slot.

## Weekly check

Every Monday, update the `Hiring pipeline` section of the weekly operating tracker from the CRM. The only recruitment numbers that count are candidates with a current next action and due date.
